# Natarinko Knowledge Base
# References

## Purpose

This document defines the standards for citing sources within the Natarinko Knowledge Base.

---

## Preferred Sources

- Peer-reviewed journals
- Academic books
- University publications
- Government publications
- International organizations
- Professional reference works

---

## Acceptable Sources

- Reputable news organizations (for current events)
- Official statistics
- Official reports

---

## Sources to Avoid

- Anonymous websites
- Unsourced blogs
- Social media posts
- Promotional material
- AI-generated content used as evidence

---

## Citation Principles

References should:

- support factual statements;
- be reliable;
- be traceable;
- be as current as appropriate for the subject.

---

## Future Standard

A unified citation style will be adopted for all encyclopedia articles.
