# Natarinko Knowledge Base
# Style Guide

## Purpose

This guide defines the writing style used throughout the Natarinko Knowledge Base.

---

## General Principles

Every article should be:

- clear;
- precise;
- objective;
- concise;
- accessible to an international audience.

---

## Language

Editors should:

- write in plain language;
- avoid unnecessary jargon;
- explain technical terms when needed;
- avoid slang and colloquialisms.

---

## Structure

A typical article should contain:

1. Introduction
2. Background
3. Main Content
4. Related Topics
5. References

---

## Formatting

- Use Markdown headings consistently.
- Prefer short paragraphs.
- Use bullet lists where appropriate.
- Use tables only when they improve clarity.

---

## Neutral Tone

Do not use promotional, emotional, or biased language.

Present information based on reliable evidence.

---

## Consistency

Use consistent terminology, spelling, capitalization, and formatting across all articles.
