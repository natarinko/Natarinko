# Taxonomy

## Purpose

This document defines the high-level classification system used throughout the Natarinko Knowledge Base.

---

## Primary Categories

- Science
- Technology
- Mathematics
- History
- Geography
- Philosophy
- Culture
- Society
- Politics
- Economics
- Law
- Medicine
- Psychology
- Education
- Linguistics
- Literature
- Arts
- Music
- Religion
- Environment

---

## Category Structure

Each primary category may contain:

- Subcategories
- Topics
- Individual articles
- Cross-references

---

## Classification Principles

Topics should be classified according to their primary subject.

If a topic belongs to multiple disciplines, one category should be designated as the primary location while related categories link to it.

---

## Navigation

The taxonomy is intended to support:

- intuitive browsing;
- efficient searching;
- consistent organization;
- future expansion.

---

## Future Development

The taxonomy will continue to evolve as new subject areas are added to the knowledge base.
