# Natarinko Knowledge Base
# Article Standard

## Purpose

This document defines the standard structure for encyclopedia articles.

---

## Every article should include

### 1. Title

Clear and unambiguous.

---

### 2. Introduction

A concise summary explaining the topic.

---

### 3. Definition

A precise definition where applicable.

---

### 4. Background

Historical or conceptual context.

---

### 5. Main Content

Detailed explanation divided into logical sections.

---

### 6. Related Topics

Links to related encyclopedia entries.

---

### 7. References

Reliable sources supporting the information.

---

## Writing Principles

Articles should be:

- accurate;
- neutral;
- verifiable;
- educational;
- logically structured.

---

## What to Avoid

- Personal opinions
- Promotional language
- Original research
- Unsupported claims
- Plagiarism

---

## Updating

Articles should be revised whenever significant new information becomes available.
