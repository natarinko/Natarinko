# Natarinko Knowledge Base
# Master Project Plan

## Vision

Natarinko is a long-term international knowledge platform designed to provide accurate, evidence-based, multilingual educational content for readers worldwide.

The project aims to combine the strengths of traditional encyclopedias, academic reference works, and modern digital knowledge systems into a single structured platform.

---

## Mission

The mission of Natarinko is to create one of the world's most trusted multilingual knowledge libraries by:

- providing accurate information;
- maintaining editorial neutrality;
- encouraging critical thinking;
- supporting lifelong learning;
- making knowledge freely accessible.

---

## Core Principles

The project follows these principles:

- Accuracy before speed.
- Evidence before opinion.
- Neutral point of view.
- Clear and accessible writing.
- Long-term educational value.
- Continuous improvement.
- International accessibility.

## Editorial Goals

Every article should be:

- accurate;
- verifiable;
- well structured;
- understandable;
- neutral;
- regularly updated.

---

## Success Criteria

The project will be considered successful when it becomes a reliable educational resource used by readers from different countries and backgrounds.
