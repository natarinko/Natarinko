# Natarinko Knowledge Base
# Editorial Policy

## Purpose

This document defines the editorial principles governing all content published within the Natarinko Knowledge Base.

---

## Core Principles

Every article must be:

- Accurate
- Neutral
- Evidence-based
- Clearly written
- Educational
- Verifiable
- Regularly maintained

---

## Editorial Standards

Editors should always:

- distinguish facts from opinions;
- use reliable sources;
- avoid sensationalism;
- write in a neutral tone;
- avoid plagiarism;
- cite sources where appropriate.

---

## Neutrality

Natarinko does not promote political, religious, ideological, or commercial viewpoints.

Articles should fairly represent significant perspectives while giving appropriate weight to evidence and scholarly consensus.

---

## Sources

Preferred sources include:

- peer-reviewed journals;
- academic books;
- official government publications;
- recognized international organizations;
- respected reference works.

Sources of uncertain reliability should not be used as primary references.

---

## Style

Editors should write:

- clearly;
- concisely;
- objectively;
- consistently;
- for an international audience.

---

## Updating Articles

Articles should be reviewed periodically to:

- correct errors;
- update information;
- improve clarity;
- improve references.

---

## Editorial Responsibility

Every contributor is responsible for maintaining the quality, neutrality, and educational value of the knowledge base.
