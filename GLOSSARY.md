# Natarinko Knowledge Base
# Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines important terms used throughout the project.

---

## Article

A structured encyclopedia entry covering a specific topic.

---

## Category

A major subject area used to organize content.

---

## Contributor

A person who creates or improves project content.

---

## Editorial Policy

The principles governing the creation and review of content.

---

## Markdown

The lightweight markup language used for writing project documents and articles.

---

## Repository

The GitHub project containing all documentation and source files.

---

## Taxonomy

The hierarchical classification system organizing knowledge across subject areas.

---

## Template

A standardized document used as the starting point for creating new articles.
